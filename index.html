How to Play

  1. Attack: pick a direction to strike. The AI tries to dodge it. Land a hit and that direction locks. Land 3 hits in a row to win.
  2. Dodge: miss, and the roles swap. Now the AI strikes and you pick a direction to dodge. Pick the same direction it strikes and you get hit.
  3. Beat the clock: run out of time and you lose the exchange. Harder difficulties give you less time.
  4. Don't be predictable: every opponent learns your habits and patterns, and each match you face a different one.
  5. Finishers: buy them in the Shop and equip them in the Inventory. Land your 3rd hit in a finisher's direction to trigger its cinematic kill. Finishers are purely cosmetic: no extra coins and no advantage.
  6. Controls: arrow keys or WASD, the buttons, or swipe on the arena.
